Blackjack

Try to get as close to 21 without going over

  • Using /dice 13 twice to draw your first 2 cards
  • The dealer will draw 1 card to simulate 1 face up and 1 face down card
  • 2 to 10 are their values | 1's are Aces equal to either 1 or 11 | 11/12/13 are Jack/Queen/King equal to 10
  • Blackjack aka a 1 + "10" with the first 2 cards is a Blackjack (an auto win unless dealer also draws blackjack)
  • The player will then choose to Hit (draw cards) or Stand (stay on current hand)
  • The player also has the option to Double Down which is to double the initial bet and draw just 1 more card
  • If the player's cards are identical, they can choose to Split, separating the cards into 2 separate hands and betting on them individually. Up to a possible 5 separate splits
  • Next the Dealer will reveal their 2nd card
  • Dealer is forced to draw cards until they reach 17+ which they are then forced to stand
  • Dealer is allowed to hit on a "soft 17" (eg. Ace + 6)
  • Win = 2x Bet | Blackjack Win = 2.5x Bet | Draw = Bet Return

Deathroll

One vs One or Group

  • Player Vs Dealer - Place your bet and take it in turns to /dice (previous roll). The first to hit "1" loses.
  • Group Deathroll - With 3 or more players the group agrees on the same bet. Using the same rules as before, taking in turns to /dice (previous roll) until someone rolls a "1" that player is out and we start again with the remaining players.
  • The winner will receive 90% of the Pot. Remaining 10% is the dealer fee.

Roulette

The higher the roll the higher the pay-out

  • Place your bet then using a single /dice try to get the highest number you can
  • Players are allowed to multi bet, for example 3 bets of 500k
  • 590+ = 2x Bet | 950+ = 3x Bet | 999 = 7x Bet

Triple Dice

Pick a lucky number and try to match the 3 dice (1-6)

  • Place your bets, pick a lucky number 1 - 6 then roll /dice 6 3 times
  • 1 match = 2x Bet | 2 Match = 3x Bet | 3 match = 5x Bet

Twenty One

Try to get as close to 21 without going over

  • Place your bet then using /dice 6 roll as many times as you wish to get as close to 21 without going over
  • The dealer will then try to match or beat your total
  • Win = 2x Bet | Draw = Bet Return
